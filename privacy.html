Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What the app accesses

With your permission, ALS reads and manages your Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive through Google's APIs, only so that you can use them through the app.

What is stored

Only the sign-in credential (an OAuth refresh token) that Google issues, kept on the developer's own devices and servers. Your mail, events and files are fetched when needed and are not copied to any other service.

Sharing

Your data is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

You can remove ALS's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
